A little block puzzle, dressed however you like.

Clear rows and columns in a cosy 8×8 grid. Every piece can wear its own tiny character — swap in the built-in Pusheen-style skins, or design your own in the separate Skin Studio.

▶ Play now ✎ Open Skin Studio

Everything about it is meant to feel gentle

No timer, no ads, no pressure — just a relaxing puzzle that quietly gets a little harder, and a set of tools to make it feel like yours.

🧩

Relaxing puzzle

Drag pieces onto an 8×8 board, clear full rows and columns, and chain combos for bonus points. Pieces never rotate, so every fit is fair.

🐾

Never a broken piece

Pieces are automatically split into straight little bodies and lone singles, redrawn fresh the instant a line clear cuts one apart — never a half-drawn fragment.

🎨

Design your own

The Skin Studio is a separate little app for uploading art, lining it up with a live seam preview, and exporting one ready-to-use skin file.

🎃

Holiday surprises

Skins can carry a start and end date. Drop a Halloween or Christmas skin in the early-skins folder and it appears only on schedule.

Making a skin, in short

One export makes one complete skin, from just eight little images. The Studio lives at studio.html, entirely separate from the game.

1
Upload art

Add a PNG for any of the eight part slots — as many or as few as you like.

2
Line it up

Drag to nudge, resize with the slider, and check every shape in the live gallery.

3
Export

Download one JSON file — art and alignment baked in together.

4
Drop it in

Place it in skins/ (or early-skins/) and list it in that folder's manifest.

Add it to your home screen

Once this is hosted (GitHub Pages works great), it opens full-screen from your home screen like any other app.

  1. 1Open this page in Safari (the home-screen option only shows up there, not in Chrome on iOS).
  2. 2Tap the Share icon in the toolbar — the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. 3Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. 4Confirm the name and tap Add. A little icon appears on your home screen and opens full-screen, no browser bar.
  1. 1Open this page in Chrome.
  2. 2Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. 3Tap Add to Home screen, then Install.
  4. 4The icon lands on your home screen and launches like a normal app.
  1. 1Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. 2Click the install icon in the address bar (or the ⋮ menu → Install Purrfect Blocks…).
  3. 3It opens in its own window from now on, with its own taskbar/dock icon.

Tip: bookmark or install the game page (game.html) itself for the fastest way in — this page is just the front door.
